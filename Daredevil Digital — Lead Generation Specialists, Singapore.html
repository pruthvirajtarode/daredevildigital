The Digital Marketing Blueprint

Six layers. Your entire marketing execution, mapped before you spend a dollar.

The audit diagnoses what's broken. The Blueprint builds what doesn't exist yet. Six forward-looking layers that give early-stage businesses a clear, actionable specification — not a generic strategy deck.

Layer 1 — Business Snapshot
Who you're selling to, what the offer is, what a qualified lead looks like. The foundation everything else is built on.
Layer 2 — Audience Architecture
Primary and secondary audience definition. Demographics, psychographics, Singapore-specific trigger events. Who Meta should find and why.
Layer 3 — Offer and Lead Mechanism
Instant Form vs landing page, friction level, qualifying questions, form copy. What you put in front of cold traffic and how it filters for quality.
Layer 4 — Campaign Blueprint
Recommended campaign structure, objective, budget allocation, ad set logic. A build-ready specification — not a live account, a plan to build one correctly.
Layer 5 — Creative Direction
Content pillars, format recommendations, hook angles for your vertical. Not production — direction. What to make, why it works, what to say in the first three seconds.
Layer 6 — 90-Day Execution Roadmap
Weeks 1–4 (launch), weeks 5–8 (optimise), weeks 9–12 (scale or adjust). Milestones, decision points, and the metrics that tell you what to do next.

What this excludes Pricing strategy, brand identity, COGS analysis, and business model advice. The Blueprint is a marketing execution document — scope is fixed by design.

FAQ

Questions we get before the audit call.

What does the S$999 audit actually cover?+
A six-layer diagnostic of your Meta account — Signal Integrity, Account Structure, Saturation, Creative, Lead Layer, and Business Layer. You get a Configuration Health score, a written list of every Critical issue with a fix attached, a Signal Loop gap analysis, and a 30-minute debrief call. If you move to a management engagement, the S$999 is credited in full against month one.
I'm not spending S$5K/month yet. Can I still work with D3?+
Yes — Option B is the right entry point. The Digital Marketing Blueprint (S$888) covers six layers: business snapshot, audience architecture, offer and lead mechanism, campaign blueprint, creative direction, and a 90-day execution roadmap. You move into Starter Management at S$699/month, with a written graduation clause: when monthly ad spend consistently clears S$3,000, you move to Option A terms.
What happens after the audit if I don't proceed to management?+
You keep the full audit report. Every Critical issue is documented with a fix — you can implement it yourself or with whoever manages your account. The audit stands alone as a diagnostic. That said, the most common outcome of seeing a Configuration Health score is wanting someone to fix it properly.
What is the Signal Loop and why does it matter?+
Most Meta lead-gen accounts optimise toward form fills. But a form fill isn't a buyer — it's just someone who clicked. The Signal Loop pushes your actual CRM outcomes (qualified lead, booked appointment, closed sale) back to Meta via CAPI and offline conversion events. Meta then trains your campaigns toward the people who buy, not just the people who fill in forms.
How does the exclusivity work exactly?+
At the Exclusive Management tier (S$3,499/month), D3 works with one business per niche. Once your niche is confirmed and you're a paying client, no direct competitor in that niche gets taken on. The slot is held for the duration of your engagement. Check the Exclusivity Tracker above to see if your niche is still open.
What access do you need to run the audit?+
View access to your Meta Business Manager and Ads Manager. No admin access, no ability to make changes. We also need 30 minutes with someone who knows your close rate and what happens to leads after they come in — without that, we can only audit half the machine.
